About Me!

Alexandra Feinberg. Education Specialist. Growing Administrator. Life-long Learner.

Welcome, and thank you for visiting my portfolio. I am an Education Specialist in the Saddleback Valley Unified School District, where I have the privilege of working alongside students, families, and colleagues every day.

My path in education began eight years ago as an ABA therapist. Working one-on-one with students taught me to look closely at what each learner needs and to celebrate every step of progress. Four years ago I transitioned into the classroom as an Education Specialist, bringing that same individualized, data-informed approach to supporting students with diverse learning needs.

I am now pursuing my administrative credential so I can support students and the educators who serve them on a broader scale. This portfolio documents my growth as an administrative candidate and the work I have completed throughout my practicum.
